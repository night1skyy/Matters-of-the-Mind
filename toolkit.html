Your Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Every tool stores data exclusively in your browser localStorage. Your mood logs, journal entries, and session records never leave your device.

Clearing browser data removes entries. For long-term records, copy important journal entries to your own notes app.

None of this data is collected, transmitted, or visible to anyone other than you.
