Helping a friend in crisis

Listen before you advise
Ask open questions. Don't minimise what they're feeling.
Don't keep it secret
If they mention self-harm, involve a trusted adult or call iCall together.
Check in again the next day
A single text — "how are you today?" — can mean everything.
Look after yourself too
Supporting someone in crisis is hard. It's okay to need support too.

Before you call — what to expect

🔒
Fully confidential

What you share stays with the counsellor. You don't need to give your real name unless you want to.

💬
No "right reason" needed

You don't have to be in crisis. Feeling anxious, overwhelmed, or just needing someone to talk to is reason enough.

🤝
Zero judgement

Counsellors are trained to listen without judgement. Share as much or as little as you're comfortable with.
